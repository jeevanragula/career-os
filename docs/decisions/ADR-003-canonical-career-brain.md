# ADR-003 — Canonical Career Brain

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-30

## Decision

Career Brain is the single canonical source for reusable professional facts.

Resumes, LinkedIn drafts, outreach, applications, interview stories, and AI summaries are derived artifacts.

No generated artifact can silently become canonical evidence.

## Rationale

Without a canonical source, each resume and AI workflow becomes a competing version of the candidate's history. That creates factual drift and makes claims difficult to verify.

## Consequences

Positive:
- one source of truth
- traceable claims
- reusable evidence
- safer tailoring
- easier correction

Trade-off:
- initial data modeling and verification require more effort.
