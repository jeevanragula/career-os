# Spec 018 — Application Tracking

## Goal
Maintain a single timeline for every opportunity and preserve outcomes for learning.

## Requirements
1. Track stage, timestamps, next action, owner, and source.
2. Record interviews, recruiter interactions, assessments, offers, rejection, withdrawal, and closure.
3. Preserve historical events rather than overwriting them.
4. Support reminders without automatically contacting third parties.
5. Feed outcomes back into future job and resume analysis.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every application has an auditable timeline.
- State transitions are validated.
- Historical events remain immutable.
