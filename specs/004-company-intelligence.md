# Specification 004 — Company Intelligence

- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Milestone: M3/M5

## Purpose

Build a structured company view for opportunity research and role-specific preparation.

## Information categories

- company identity
- product and market
- business model
- funding/public-company context
- leadership
- engineering organization
- technology signals
- security/data/AI relevance
- recent public developments
- open roles
- relevant outreach people

## Source policy

Public company facts retain source references and observation timestamps. Contested or interpretive claims are attributed to their source.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Company records aggregate multiple public sources.
- [ ] Facts have source references.
- [ ] Time-sensitive observations have timestamps.
- [ ] Research does not silently convert speculation into fact.
- [ ] Outreach people have source context.
