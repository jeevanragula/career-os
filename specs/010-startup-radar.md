# Specification 010 — Startup Radar

- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Milestone: M5

## Purpose

Identify startups and growth-stage companies in AI infrastructure, AI security, cloud security, data security, and data infrastructure.

## Target stages

Primary focus: Series A, Series B, Series C. Seed and pre-seed opportunities may be tracked separately because risk, role scope, and public information differ.

## Target roles

- Founding Engineer
- Founding Member of Engineering
- Principal Engineer
- Staff / Senior Staff Engineer
- Lead Architect
- Platform Architect
- Head of Platform
- Principal Security Engineer
- Founding AI Engineer

## Research dimensions

company, product, technical problem, funding stage, public funding information, founders, CTO/engineering leadership, relevant roles, candidate-to-company evidence, outreach targets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Startup records include source references.
- [ ] Funding information is timestamped.
- [ ] Role targets are normalized.
- [ ] Candidate relevance is evidence-based.
- [ ] Research does not invent private company facts.
