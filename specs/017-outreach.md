# Spec 017 — Personalized Outreach

## Goal
Draft concise, evidence-backed outreach for recruiters, hiring managers, founders, and professional contacts.

## Requirements
1. Message context comes from job/company evidence and Career Brain claims.
2. No invented relationship, referral, interaction, or achievement.
3. Each factual sentence traces to evidence.
4. AI generates drafts only.
5. Sending requires explicit human approval.
6. Contact data must be obtained from permitted sources.

## Acceptance criteria
- Drafts include claim references.
- No message can transition to sent without approval.
- Duplicate outreach is detected for the same recipient/job/context.
