# Spec 020 — Startup Radar

## Goal
Discover relevant startup opportunities using the same evidence and provenance discipline as job discovery.

## Requirements
1. Track startup identity, source, stage, location, business area, technical signals, and open roles.
2. Separate observed facts from AI-generated interpretation.
3. Score relevance using transparent criteria, never a hidden overall judgment.
4. Preserve source URLs and observation timestamps.
5. Support founder/team outreach drafts with human approval.

## Acceptance criteria
- Startup records retain source provenance.
- Role matches link back to canonical jobs where possible.
- AI interpretation is clearly distinguished from source facts.
