# Specification 009 — Interview OS

- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Milestone: M8

## Purpose

Generate job-specific interview preparation from the job description, company research, and verified Career Brain evidence.

## Preparation areas

- behavioral questions
- technical questions
- distributed systems
- Kubernetes and cloud
- security architecture
- data-platform architecture
- AI and agent architecture
- system design
- candidate questions for interviewers

## Rules

Preparation may identify likely topics and create practice questions, but it must distinguish sourced company information from inference.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Preparation links to a specific opportunity.
- [ ] Candidate stories trace to verified evidence.
- [ ] Company facts have source references.
- [ ] Practice feedback is separate from canonical career facts.
